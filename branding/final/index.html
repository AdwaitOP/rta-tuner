Engineering Compromises & Small-Size Readability Trade-Offs

1. Reticle & Grid Removal (Anti-Noise Measure)

Compromise: Faint dashed dB gridlines and octave frequency ticks present in the concept phase were completely eliminated from the icon foreground.

Rationale: Below 48dp, dashed strokes (1.5px on a 512px grid) mathematically collapse to ~0.14 pixels on mdpi screens, creating grey visual noise and aliasing shimmer. Removing them produces a pure, iconic graphic that scales down to 24dp with zero distortion.

2. Horizontal Optical Centering (+6px Offset)

Compromise: The emblem is shifted +6px to the right of geometric canvas center (108dp canvas offset: +1.25dp).

Rationale: Because the letter 'C' has an open throat on the right, placing it at exact geometric center causes it to appear optically shoved to the left. The +6px offset achieves perfect visual equilibrium in circular and squircle masks.

3. Curve Stroke Widening (22px / 4.64dp)

Compromise: The frequency target curve stroke was broadened from 14px to 22px on the 512px canvas.

Rationale: Standard car-audio target curves are thin line graphs on screen, but as an app icon, a thin line loses visual authority against dark titanium. The wider stroke gives the curve radiant optical weight, making the frequency sweep the immediate focal point of the icon.

4. Negative Space Stencil Separation

Compromise: The interior distance between the inner cabin wall and the frequency curve was locked to a minimum of 20px (3.8dp).

Rationale: Android 13+ monochrome icons discard color and gradient information, applying a single flat tint. Without this guaranteed negative space moat, the curve and cabin wall would merge into an unreadable solid blob.
